DESCRIPTION

chinmay is Chinmay Bhadoria, one of the three co-founders of Zextiria AI, with Aarv Singh Chauhan and Ayush Bhatt. Chinmay and Aarv write the code and design the products; Ayush works with the clients. Based in Gwalior, India, building full time alongside college.

It started at a hackathon. Only one of us had the paid AI subscription, so we could paste what the AI wrote, but we couldn't hand each other the agent.

Then came AgentinFlow, our own studio for web builds and automation. Every client got a scope document, but the decisions made later, on calls and in messages, were never written down. The client saw the final result; the decisions made along the way were lost.

Zextiria Rooms is the fix: project rooms where AI drafts and clients approve.

01 rooms.zextiriaai.com

Zextiria Rooms

Multiplayer AI for teams that do not share an org.

A room holds one document, the people from both companies, and the agents each side brings. An agent proposes a change. A named person with the authority to accept it approves it, and the reasoning stays on the record. Guests arrive from a link with no account and no setup, which is the only way the invite actually gets used.

Agent owners connect the assistant they already run over MCP. Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Gemini all speak it, and they bring their own context, tools and model costs with them.
